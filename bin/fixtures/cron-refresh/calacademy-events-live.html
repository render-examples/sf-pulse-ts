Daily Calendar

Iconic exhibits

Steinhart Aquarium

Come face-to-fin with nearly 60,000 animals from 800+ species—many of which are displayed nowhere else on Earth.

Morrison Planetarium

The interior of Morrison Planetarium—one of the world's largest all-digital domes.

Experience the cosmos from within our immersive 75-foot planetarium dome. Reserve a showtime when you arrive.

Osher Rainforest

Journey through a living 4-story rainforest, home to free-flying butterflies and birds and giant Amazonian fish.

Kimball Natural History Museum

image-large

Explore hands-on exhibits, fascinating specimens, and the latest research conducted by Academy scientists.

Ongoing exhibits

California: State of Nature

A multi-sensory exhibition reveals the surprising ways people, places, and species are connected in California.

Venom: Fangs, Stingers, and Spines

Encounter an eye-catching array of 12+ venomous species, from scorpions and jellies to spiders and snakes.

Twilight Zone: Deep Reefs Revealed

Life in technicolor: Neon-hued fish and corals abound in Twilight Zone’s vivid habitats.

Candy colors. Ingenious adaptations. Extreme conditions. Meet the unusual animals that inhabit deep reefs.

BigPicture: Natural World Photography

Grand Prize winner: Lemur’s Tough Life by Donglin Zhou

Embark on a visual odyssey of 2025's best nature, wildlife, and conservation photography. Exhibit open daily.

Hidden Wonders: Inside the Academy's Collections

"Kitty," our California saber-toothed cat (Smilodon californicus) fossil, was collected from the La Brea Tar Pits in Los Angeles.

Peek into the past in an interactive exhibition of nearly 800 objects from the Academy's research collections.

Shake House

Step in and hang on as our earthquake simulator recreates California's powerful 1906 and 1989 quakes.

Gems and Minerals Unearthed

Marvel at nearly 400 dazzling and dramatic gem and mineral specimens from the Academy's geology collection.

Academy in Action

image-large

Our colorful new mini-exhibition brings our three regeneration initiatives out of the field and into the museum.

Color of Life

Live animals and hands-on exhibits reveal the significant role color plays across a vibrant spectrum of species.

California Coast

Get a glimpse of the richness right off our coast and gaze at rockfish, eels, anemones, sharks, and more.

New Science: The Academy Exhibit

image-large

Celebrate LGBTQ+ women and gender minorities of color working in STEMM in an exhibit kiosk and online gallery.

Philippine Coral Reef

image-large

Watch reef fish dart through colorful forests of coral while giant rays sail gracefully under your feet.

Tusher African Hall

image-large

Explore our evolutionary history while surrounded by dioramas of African wildlife and splashing penguins.

Living Roof

image-large

Rolling hills and 1.7 million plants create an oasis for native species atop the Academy's 2.5-acre living roof.

Human Odyssey

image-large

Trace the milestones of our species’ fascinating history by studying skull casts, interactive maps, and more.

Water Planet

image-large

Discover how animals have evolved fascinating methods of living in and out of water.

Hands-on exhibits

Nature Lab

Welcome to our newly reimagined hub for hands-on exploration, with touchable specimens, science games, and more.

Wander Woods

Activate your kids' curiosity about the natural world in our outdoor nature play and exploration space.

Curiosity Grove

Young imaginations soar in this colorful, California forest-themed playspace. Open daily, 10 a.m.–4 p.m.
